## Investigating the earlier timeout

On the historical phone run, 22 of 23 tests passed. The stack points to line 212 at `8ccd87df`, the ten-second wait after the confirmation action. That log has no busy, dialog or persistence observation at the timeout. The unchanged full rerun passed 23/23 and three independent focused runs passed. The historical failure remains unexplained. [Historical record](validation/backup-ranking/historical.json) · [Issue #35](https://github.com/moikdev/droidcast/issues/35)

The ranking test now reports the dialog, busy and error flags, lifecycle and window focus, pending document presence, and whether the model and preference value reached the maximum. A failure inside the confirmation protocol emits a compact instrumentation status. Opt-in evidence saves observations around confirmation, the first preference notification time, and app screenshots with their hierarchies. The notification and preference read describe in-process state. They do not measure disk flush duration. A separate collection regression checks persistence after reopening the store.
